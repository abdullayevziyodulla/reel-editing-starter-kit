# The editing method

## Start with meaning, then time

Read the transcript before selecting b-roll. Keep one clear idea per sentence. Remove redundant starts, long pauses, filler only where it does not damage natural speech, and repeats. A good hook can be a later sentence moved to the front, but never reorder into a misleading claim. Keep enough audio padding for consonants and breathing. All alternatives need separate jobs.

The source of truth has three layers:

1. `segments.json`: what the speaker said, indexed by segment ID, with raw source times.
2. `words.json`: the same spelling with estimated raw word start/end times. Gemini is authoritative for Uzbek spelling; Whisper is used for timing only.
3. `pieces.json`: padded/frame-snapped source intervals in **chosen playback order**, generated by render.py.

Do not use the expected transcript duration as the rendered duration. FFmpeg/ffprobe determine the actual base duration; account for frame rounding.

Example: a segment starts at raw 20.0s, its kept piece starts at 19.9s, previous pieces total 8.0s, and a word starts at raw 21.2s. That word appears at `8.0 + 21.2 - 19.9 = 9.3s`. It should trigger its matching card or cut around 9.3s, not 21.2s. `toolkit.py timing` exports these mappings, sorted by output time, and includes segment bounds.

## Dynamic treatment

- Hook: concise title/text_hook on a gently enlarged face or an actual short demonstration.
- List: one relevant image per spoken item; full-screen or split shots with restrained shutter/click sounds. Order follows the spoken list, not the download order.
- Demonstration: screen recording/GIF at the top and visible speaker below. Move captions away from the actual mouth; the split seam can work if there is room.
- Explanation: return to the speaker; alternate modest zoom levels by sentence. Large punch-ins often cut the hair.
- Qualification: stamp/check or a small logo card matched to the word which explains the limitation.
- CTA: one clear comment/action, timed to the spoken request. Keep it brief and deliver the promised guide/link separately.

## Clean motion-card treatment

Use a quiet light or dark canvas, readable card typography, one clear animation per idea, and a visible complete head below. Speaker source scale and the card width are independent; the card must be narrower than the scaled video. A person segmentation mask allows the head to sit above the card rather than cropping its top. The smoothing mask must copy the native numpy view before retaining it.

Cards should fill the sentence with meaning, not just decoration. Use a counter for a number, tiles for a list, a chat for a question/answer, stack for related sources, strike for a misconception, checklist for facts, converge for bringing data together, comment for the CTA, and window for demonstrations. Chip positions near the bottom can overlap captions: check actual frames, keep bottom chips above caption_y with a useful margin.

Dark mode uses canvas #0E0F12 and cards #1A1C21 with white primary and muted secondary text. Keep accent colors. White capsule captions need dark text in **both** themes and face mode.

## Timing intros and outros

An intro_duration value only shifts captions; it does not prepend media. If adding a silent 1.5s visual opener, first make a new base with 1.5s frozen source frames and audio delayed by 1.5s, then cover it with the chosen b-roll. Set intro_duration=1.5; export timing with `--intro 1.5`, and write all scene/SFX times on the shifted timeline. Keep pieces.json as the speech-only trim map. Validate layout against the actual longer base.

For a frozen CTA outro, extend video and append silence; do not stretch the narration. Word times remain unchanged. Extend layout/graphics to the actual base duration. These extensions are advanced media operations; the kit deliberately does not pretend that a JSON field alone changes the video.

## Audio

The compositor normalizes narration, mixes music with sidechain ducking, adds SFX and limits the mix. This is a preliminary mix. The final integrated loudness/true peak can differ after adding beds, SFX and AAC encoding. Use the generic finalizer on the **complete** rendered video and inspect its verification report.

Use no-vocal music under narration. Start around -16 dB in these example plans, listen on headphones and phone speakers, and adjust based on the music master. Small UI sounds should mark an actual visual action, not every word. Do not drown out Uzbek consonants or make the listener work to understand the speaker.

## Before delivery

Preview/check frames are mandatory. Inspect the first frame, each scene entry/settled state, b-roll changes, important word anchors, caption transitions and CTA. Then listen to the full mix. After the full render, finalize and decode-check; extract final frames if anything changed. Report verified duration and audio figures. A validation pass is necessary but is not visual approval.

For covers, use a real original identity reference, central crop-safe subject/text and correct official logos. Reattaching the original identity photo helps prevent drift across repeated image edits. Verify hands and face width rather than assuming the image generator respected the prompt.
